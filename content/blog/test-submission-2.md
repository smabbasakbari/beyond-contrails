There are some flights that feel more significant than others. Riyadh Air's RX659 from Riyadh to Islamabad was one of them. On 14 August 2026, Pakistan's Independence Day, Riyadh Air launched its new service between Saudi Arabia and Pakistan, and we recreated the inaugural flight in Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

For this flight, I operated **Riyadh Air RX659 (RXI659)** aboard the Boeing 787-9 **HZ-RXAG**, departing from King Khalid International Airport in Riyadh and flying to Islamabad International Airport. The flight was conducted live on **VATSIM**, with live network traffic and ATC, while BeyondATC supplemented the environment with real-world traffic based on FlightRadar24 data.

## The Flight

The aircraft departed **Riyadh (OERK) from Runway 33R** and followed the **RAPM2D departure**, continuing via RAPMA before joining the route towards Pakistan. The total recorded distance was **1,755 nautical miles**, with a real flight time of **3 hours 38 minutes** and a block time of **3 hours 54 minutes**.

The flight was operated with **338 passengers** onboard, making for a particularly busy and realistic long-haul operation. Across the flight, the 787-9 consumed approximately **19,700 kg of fuel**.

The aircraft reached a maximum recorded cruise altitude of approximately **40,000 ft**, settling into a long cruise across the region before beginning the descent towards Islamabad.

## Arrival into Islamabad

The arrival was completed smoothly, with the aircraft touching down at a recorded **126 knots**. The landing produced a **\-165.96 ft/min landing rate**, with a recorded touchdown **G-force of 1.02 G** — a very respectable result for the inaugural sector.

The flight recorder recorded the aircraft as being **on blocks at 14:48 BST**, compared with the scheduled on-block time of **19:00 BST**. The recorded off-block time was **15:30 BST**, against a scheduled departure of **15:25 BST**. The flight was therefore recorded as departing **5 minutes after schedule**, with the tracking data recording the flight as **early** overall. The displayed block and on-block timestamps appear to reflect differing time references, so the recorded flight and block durations are the more useful measures of the actual simulator flight.

## A Significant New Route

What made RX659 particularly special was not simply the aircraft or the destination. This was a recreation of **Riyadh Air's inaugural Riyadh–Islamabad service**, operated on **Pakistan's Independence Day**.

The route represents another step in Riyadh Air's rapidly expanding international network and strengthens the growing air links between Saudi Arabia and Pakistan. Flying the inaugural sector virtually, with live VATSIM ATC and real-world traffic simulation, provided an opportunity to recreate the operation as closely as possible within Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

From pushback at Riyadh to touchdown in Islamabad, RX659 was a memorable sector — and one that will be particularly interesting to revisit as Riyadh Air's network continues to grow.
